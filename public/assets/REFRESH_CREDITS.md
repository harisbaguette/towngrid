Kenney original GLBs were repackaged with the original palette image embedded. Quaternius farm OBJ/MTL files were repackaged to GLB with original geometry, normals, and diffuse materials; the wind pump's rotor was preserved as a separate object with a centered pivot. Robot and Horse were converted from original FBX to GLB, retaining the rig and authored animations. Original names and detailed clip metadata are in `manifest.json`. No meshes were synthesized to substitute for missing source assets. Audio is unchanged from the creator downloads.

Creator license files and source declarations are retained under `licenses/`. No Town Star assets, artwork, code, or audio are included.
